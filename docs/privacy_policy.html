Privacy Policy

App: Journal of Dream

Last updated: March 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Journal of Dream ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects user data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account information — such as email address, display name, and Firebase user ID.
  • User-generated content — such as dream entries, locations, comments, votes, likes, and preferences.
  • Technical and diagnostics data — such as crash logs, device metadata related to app crashes, app stability data, and diagnostic information.
  • Analytics data — such as in-app interactions and usage events used to improve the App.
  • Local app data — such as settings, selected language, and local security-related state stored on your device.

3. How We Use Data

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To authenticate users and allow access to account-based features.
  • To save and sync dreams, locations, settings, comments, and interactions.
  • To improve app reliability, monitor crashes, and fix technical issues.
  • To understand how users interact with the App and improve features and usability.
  • To protect the App from abuse and maintain normal operation.

4. Services Used

The App may use Google Firebase services, including but not limited to:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase services may process certain data necessary for authentication, synchronization, analytics, diagnostics, and app stability. Firebase provides guidance for developers on Play Data safety disclosures, but the final responsibility for accurate disclosure belongs to the developer. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

5. Data Storage

Some data is stored locally on your device. Other data is stored in cloud infrastructure used by the App, including Firebase services.

6. Public Comments and Interactions

If you post comments in public sections of the App, those comments may be visible to other users.

If you delete your account, some public comments may remain visible in anonymized form in order to preserve discussion structure, but they will no longer remain linked to your active account.

7. Account Deletion

If the App allows account creation, the App also provides an in-app option to request account deletion. When an account deletion request is processed, associated account data such as dreams, locations, settings, and linked account records are deleted. Some public comments may remain only in anonymized form. Google Play requires a readily discoverable way to request deletion both inside and outside the app. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

8. External Deletion Request Page

An external account deletion page is provided by the developer for users who cannot access the App but still want to request deletion of their account and associated data.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data to advertisers. Data may be processed by service providers strictly for operating the App, such as authentication, cloud storage, analytics, and crash reporting.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user data. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children unless explicitly stated otherwise in the store listing.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available through the developer’s published policy page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or account deletion, contact the developer using the support contact listed on the app’s store page or deletion page.
