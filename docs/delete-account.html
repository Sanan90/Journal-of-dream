Delete Account

App: Journal of Dream

Last updated: March 11, 2026

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open Journal of Dream.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

What is deleted

  • Account data
  • Dream entries
  • Locations
  • Private settings linked to the account
  • Other associated account data stored by the app

What may remain

Public comments may remain in anonymized form as comments from a deleted user, in order to preserve discussion structure, but they will no longer be linked to your account.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access the app, use the support contact published on the app listing and include the email address of your account so the developer can process your request where applicable.
