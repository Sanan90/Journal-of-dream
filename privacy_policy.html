🌙 Privacy Policy

Dream Journal (Дневник снов) · Last updated: March 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Dream Journal ("we", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the app's functionality:

  • Account data: email address and display name when you register or sign in with Google.
  • Dream records: titles, descriptions, dates, and categories of dreams you create.
  • Dream locations: names and descriptions of locations you associate with dreams.
  • App settings: notification preferences, selected language, and PIN protection status (stored locally).
  • Crash reports: anonymous technical data about app crashes via Firebase Crashlytics to help us improve stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sync your dream diary across your devices via Firebase Firestore.
  • To send optional daily reminders and motivational quotes (only if enabled by you).
  • To display your personal statistics and achievements within the app.
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs using anonymized crash data.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore), which provides industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Each user's data is accessible only to that user — enforced via Firebase Security Rules tied to their authenticated account.

Dream data is also stored locally on your device using Android Room database for offline access.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, cloud storage, crash reporting
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your dream records are private and visible only to you.

6. Guest Mode

You may use the app without creating an account ("guest mode"). In this case, all data is stored only on your device and is not synced to the cloud.

7. Data Deletion

You may delete your data at any time by deleting individual records within the app, or by deleting your account. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
